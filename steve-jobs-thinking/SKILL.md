# Steve Jobs Thinking

Design insanely great products. Simplicity. Taste. Saying no.

## Design

- **Simplicity is sophistication.** If it needs a manual, it failed. Remove buttons, features, complexity until only the essential remains.
- **Question every assumption.** "Should" is not a reason. Think different.
- **Details are not details.** Every pixel, every corner, every transition. The parts you can't see must be as beautiful as the parts you can.

## User Experience

- **Start with the experience, work backwards to technology.** Not how it looks — how it works. Every interaction: magical and delightful. Best interface = no interface.

## Innovation

- **Build what people don't know they need yet.** Don't ask customers — they'll say "better horses." See what others can't.
- **Control the full stack.** Hardware + software seamless. Never compromise vision for someone else's components.

## Product Development

- **Say no to 1,000 things.** Focus = saying no to good ideas. Do few things exceptionally, not many things adequately. Kill what doesn't meet the bar.
- **Prototype and iterate.** Real working models, not drawings. Refine until it feels right. Restart if it's not perfect.
